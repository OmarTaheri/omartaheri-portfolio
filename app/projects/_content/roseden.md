## Overview

Rose Den is a season-led floral storefront built around the idea that flowers should match the person and the moment. The experience combines bouquet shopping, custom-event discovery, and a distinctive editorial identity in one responsive site.

## What I built

- A motion-rich landing experience with a drifting cloud of real flower imagery.
- A seasonal bouquet catalogue with product pages and a persistent shopping bag.
- Story, contact, and custom-event journeys that keep the brand voice consistent.
- A responsive interface designed to feel expressive without getting in the way of shopping.
- A Cloudflare-compatible application build.

## Technologies

- Next.js
- React
- TypeScript
- vinext
- Cloudflare Workers

## Design direction

The visual language balances generous white space, expressive typography, photography, and restrained motion. The result feels like an independent florist's editorial lookbook while still behaving like a practical storefront.
