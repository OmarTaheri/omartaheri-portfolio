## Why I built it

This project started with a wonderfully dangerous thought. I found someone selling a simpler version of this kind of app, looked at what I knew I could build, and asked myself: **“Why not build it?”**

So I did.

What began as a tier-list editor grew into a real product: a searchable marketplace, accounts, creator profiles, public rankings, collaborative rooms, analytics, subscriptions, advertising tools, and a separate moderation dashboard. It keeps answering “one more feature” with another late night—and I mean that affectionately.

## What it can do

- Build tier lists with customizable rows and smooth drag-and-drop controls.
- Explore templates across sports, gaming, anime, movies, music, food, technology, and more.
- Save, publish, share, and favorite boards from a personal dashboard.
- Rank together through synchronized Liveblocks rooms, cursors, and remote dragging.
- Give creators public profiles, templates, and analytics.
- Give moderators tools for users, reports, content, ads, plans, and platform settings.

## Built with

- Next.js 16, React 19, and TypeScript
- PostgreSQL and Drizzle ORM
- Better Auth
- Liveblocks and CRDT-backed board state
- dnd-kit
- Stripe and FlyDrive

## The idea that stayed

The best part of a tier list is that it turns taste into conversation. I wanted that conversation to feel fast, social, and polished—whether two friends are ranking Pokémon or a whole community is choosing the greatest movie of all time.

If this project gives you an idea, build it. “Why not?” has been a surprisingly productive question.
