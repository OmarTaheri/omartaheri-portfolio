## Overview

Floussi Merchant is a Kotlin mobile application for managing money, created during the CIH Hackathon.

## Product focus

- Make common merchant money-management tasks approachable on mobile.
- Keep the experience focused enough to design and build within a hackathon.
- Translate a financial workflow into a clear application interface.

## Technologies

- Kotlin
- Android application development
- Mobile product design
