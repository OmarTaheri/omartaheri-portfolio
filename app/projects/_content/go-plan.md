## Overview

Go Plan is an AI-powered degree planner for students at Al Akhawayn University. It explores how a planning assistant can make a complicated academic path easier to understand.

## Product focus

- Keep degree planning centered on the student’s academic context.
- Turn a long planning process into a clearer sequence of choices.
- Use AI as an assistant for exploration rather than as a replacement for official academic guidance.

## Technologies

- AI-assisted planning
- Web application development
- Education-focused product design
